Common Questions

Before You Call, a Few Things to Know

How fast can an engineer actually get to me?

For genuine emergencies no heat, no hot water, a suspected gas leak our average arrival time is under 60 minutes, 24 hours a day. Next-day and routine visits are booked to a fixed window so you're never left guessing.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. Every job is quoted upfront based on the response tier you choose, before any work begins. There are no surprise call-out fees added afterwards.

Are your engineers actually Gas Safe registered?

Every engineer we send is Gas Safe registered and ID checked, and our business is also OFTEC registered for oil-fired systems. You can ask to see an engineer's ID card on arrival.

Do you offer reduced rates for landlords or OAPs?

Yes we offer VIP and OAP rates from £65+VAT, along with landlord safety certificates (CP12) and cylinder servicing at a reduced rate. Ask when you book.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

All completed work is backed by a 1 year warranty. If something isn't right, call us and we'll come back to put it right at no extra cost.
